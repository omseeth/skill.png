## Question
Let \(\mathbb I=\{0,1\}\), so \(\mathbb I^n=\{0,1\}^n\). Equip \(\mathbb I^n\) with the Hamming metric \(d(x,y)=|\{j:x(j)\neq y(j)\}|\). For each integer \(n\ge 1\) and each \(k=0,1,\dots,n\), let \(S_k\) denote the \(k\)-spherical averaging operator on functions \(f:\mathbb I^n\to\mathbb C\), defined by
\[
S_kf(x)=\frac{1}{\binom{n}{k}}\sum_{\substack{y\in\mathbb I^n\\ d(x,y)=k}} f(y).
\]
For a finite sequence \((g_k)_{k=0}^n\), define its pointwise \(r\)-variation by
\[
V_r(g_k:0\le k\le n)(x)=\sup_{0\le k_0<\cdots<k_J\le n}\Big(\sum_{j=1}^J |g_{k_j}(x)-g_{k_{j-1}}(x)|^r\Big)^{1/r}.
\]
What is the strongest valid statement about the \(\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)\) size of \(V_r(S_kf:0\le k\le n)\), uniformly over all dimensions \(n\), when \(r\in[1,\infty)\)?

## Choices
A. For every \(r\in[1,\infty)\) and every \(n\ge 1\), there exists a nonzero \(f\in\mathbb C^{\mathbb I^n}\) such that \[\|V_r(S_kf:k\in\{0,1,\dots,n\})\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}\ge 2n^{1/r}\,\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}.\]
B. For every \(r\in(2,\infty)\), \[\sup_n\sup_{f\in\mathbb C^{\mathbb I^n}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{\|V_r(S_kf:k\in\{0,1,\dots,n\})\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}{\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}<\infty,\] and the failure of uniform bounds can occur only in the range \(1\le r\le 2\).
C. For every \(r\in[1,\infty)\), \[\sup_n\sup_{f\in\mathbb C^{\mathbb I^n}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{\|V_r(S_kf:k\in\{0,1,\dots,n\})\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}{\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}=\infty.\]
D. For every \(r\in[1,\infty)\), there exists \(\delta_r>0\) such that for all sufficiently large \(n\), \[\sup_{f\in\mathbb C^{\mathbb I^n}\setminus\{0\}}\frac{\|V_r(S_kf:k\in\{0,1,\dots,n\})\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}{\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}}\le (2-\delta_r)\,n^{1/r}.\]
E. For every \(r\in[1,\infty)\), there exists a constant \(C_r<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\) and every \(f\in\mathbb C^{\mathbb I^n}\), \[\|V_r(S_kf:k\in\{0,1,\dots,n\})\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}\le C_r\,\frac{n^{1/r}}{\log(n+2)}\,\|f\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb I^n)}.\]
