## Question
Let $A$ be a finite abelian group, let $V_A=A\oplus \widehat{A}$ where $\widehat{A}$ is the Pontryagin dual of $A$, and consider the associated Clifford extension
\[
1 \longrightarrow V_A \longrightarrow C(A) \longrightarrow \Sp(V_A) \longrightarrow 1,
\]
where $C(A)$ is the Clifford group and $\Sp(V_A)$ is the symplectic group of $V_A$. Which of the following statements is equivalent to this exact sequence splitting as a semidirect product?

## Choices
A. $|A|$ is odd.
B. The exact sequence splits if and only if the $2$-primary component of $A$ is cyclic.
C. The exact sequence splits if and only if $A$ has no direct summand isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_{2^k}$ for any $k\ge 2$.
D. $4 \nmid |A|$, i.e. the order of $A$ is not divisible by $4$.
E. The exact sequence splits if and only if the $2$-primary component of $A$ is elementary abelian; equivalently, it has exponent $2$.
