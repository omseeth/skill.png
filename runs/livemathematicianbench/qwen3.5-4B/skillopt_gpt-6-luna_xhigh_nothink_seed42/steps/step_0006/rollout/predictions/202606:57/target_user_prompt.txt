## Question
For each integer \(n\ge 1\), let \(\delta_n\) denote the maximum density of a sphere packing in \(\mathbf{R}^n\), and let \(\delta_n^L\) denote the maximum density of a lattice sphere packing in \(\mathbf{R}^n\) (that is, a sphere packing whose centers form a lattice). Which of the following is the strongest statement of the form “there exists a constant such that for all integers \(n\ge 1\)” describing their uniform relationship?

## Choices
A. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\), \[\delta_n\ge \delta_n^L\ge c\,n\,2^{-n}.\]
B. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\), \[\delta_n\ge \delta_n^L \quad\text{and}\quad \delta_n-\delta_n^L\ge c\,n^2\,2^{-n}.\]
C. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\), \[\delta_n\ge (1+c\,n^2\,2^{-n})\,\delta_n^L \quad\text{and}\quad \delta_n^L\ge c\,n^2\,2^{-n}.\]
D. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\), \[\delta_n>\delta_n^L\ge c\,n^2\,2^{-n}.\]
E. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for every integer \(n\ge 1\), \[\delta_n\ge \delta_n^L\ge c\,n^2\,2^{-n}.\]
