## Question
Fix an integer $k\ge 2$. Let $S_{2k}$ be the space of holomorphic cusp forms of weight $2k$ and level $1$, and let $B_{2k}$ be an orthogonal basis of Hecke eigenforms normalized by
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n\ge 1} a_f(n)\, n^{\frac{2k-1}{2}} e^{2\pi i n z},\qquad a_f(1)=1.
\]
For such $f$, define
\[
A_f(1,n)=\sum_{d^2\mid n} a_f\bigl((n/d^2)^2\bigr).
\]
For a Schwartz function $g\in \mathcal S(0,\infty)$, with Mellin transform $\widetilde g(s)=\int_0^\infty g(x)x^{s-1}\,dx$, and an integer $\ell\ge 1$, set
\[
I_\ell(g)=\sideset{}{^h}\sum_{f\in B_{2k}} a_f(\ell)\sum_{n=1}^\infty A_f(1,n)g(n),
\]
where $\sideset{}{^h}\sum$ denotes the sum weighted by the reciprocal of the Petersson norm. Let $\mu=(1-2k,2k-1,0)$, and define the transform $\mathcal T_\mu$ by
\[
(\mathcal T_\mu g)(y)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{(\sigma)} \widetilde g(1-s)\,\mathcal G_\mu(s)\,y^{-s}\,ds,\qquad \sigma>0.
\]
For an integer $\ell\ge 1$, which strongest identity holds for the associated quantity $I_\ell(g)$?

## Choices
A. For every \(g\in\mathcal S\) and every \(\ell\ge 1\), the off-diagonal Kloosterman part in the Petersson trace expansion of \(I_\ell(g)\) is already invariant under \(g\mapsto \mathcal T_\mu g\); consequently, the identity \(I_\ell(g)=I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\) follows by matching only the diagonal terms.
B. For every \(g\in\mathcal S\) and every \(\ell\ge 1\), one has \(I_\ell(g)=I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\) whenever \(\ell\) is not a perfect square, while for \(\ell=m^2\) the two sides differ precisely by the \(n=\pm 2m\) singular terms arising in the Poisson-summed Kloosterman contribution.
C. For every \(g\in\mathcal S\) and every \(\ell\ge 1\), one has \(I_\ell(g)=I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\).
D. For every \(g\in\mathcal S\) and every non-square \(\ell\ge 1\), one has \(I_\ell(g)=I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\).
E. There exists a choice of \(\sigma>0\), depending at most on \(k\) but not on \(g\) or \(\ell\), such that \(I_\ell(g)=I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\) holds for that contour, but the value of \(I_\ell(\mathcal T_\mu g)\) can change if one replaces \(\sigma\) by a different positive real number in the Mellin integral defining \(\mathcal T_\mu\).
