## Question
Let \(F\subseteq \mathbb{R}^p\) be a connected self-similar set with finitely many directions, meaning that in a defining iterated function system for \(F\), the orthogonal parts of the similarities generate a finite subgroup of the orthogonal group. Assume that \(\dim_H F=s>1\), and fix \(\alpha\in(0,1]\). Consider a generic function \(f:F\to\mathbb{R}\) in the Baire-category sense among those satisfying the \(\alpha\)-Hölder bound \(|f(x)-f(y)|\le |x-y|^\alpha\) for all \(x,y\in F\). What is the strongest statement that can be proved about the upper box dimension of the level set \(f^{-1}(r)\) for Lebesgue almost every \(r\in f(F)\)?

## Choices
A. For a generic such \(f\) and for Lebesgue almost every \(r\in f(F)\), one has \(\overline{\dim}_B f^{-1}(r)< s-\alpha\).
B. For a generic such \(f\) and for Lebesgue almost every \(r\in f(F)\), one has \(\overline{\dim}_B f^{-1}(r)\ge s-\alpha\).
C. For a generic such \(f\) and for every \(r\in f(F)\), one has \(\overline{\dim}_B f^{-1}(r)=s-\alpha\).
D. For every such \(f\) and for Lebesgue almost every \(r\in f(F)\), one has \(\overline{\dim}_B f^{-1}(r)=s-\alpha\).
E. For a generic such \(f\) and for Lebesgue almost every \(r\in f(F)\), one has \(\overline{\dim}_B f^{-1}(r)=s-\alpha\).
