## Question
Let \(M\) be the link of the Lawson--Osserman cone in \(\mathbb{R}^7\), i.e. the intersection of that cone with the unit sphere \(\mathbb{S}^6\subset \mathbb{R}^7\). Which description gives the complete classification of Jacobi fields on \(M\)?

## Choices
A. They are exactly the Killing Jacobi fields; every Jacobi field on \(M\) is a Killing Jacobi field.
B. They are exactly the infinitesimal deformations that extend to genuine one-parameter families of nearby minimal cones; in this case, that space consists of the Killing Jacobi fields together with one additional integrable normal mode represented by the first-eigenfield \(e_4\).
C. They are exactly the \(0\)-eigenvalue fields of the Jacobi operator, but this \(17\)-dimensional kernel is strictly larger than the Killing part: it also contains non-Killing Jacobi fields arising from the commuting \(L\)-\(\widetilde L\) decomposition.
D. They are precisely the normal eigenfields in the nonpositive spectrum of the Jacobi operator; for this link, that means the direct sum of the eigenspaces with eigenvalues \(-\tfrac{15}{4}\), \(-3\), and \(0\).
E. Every Killing Jacobi field on \(M\) is indeed a Jacobi field.
