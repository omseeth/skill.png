## Question
Let $N\ge 4$, let $0\le s_2<s_1<2$, let $\Omega\subset \mathbb{R}^N$ be a bounded domain, let $\lambda$ be a fixed parameter, and let $\Sigma\subset\Omega$ be a smooth compact submanifold of dimension $2\le k\le N-2$. Let $h\in C(\Omega)$ be such that the operator $-\Delta+h$ is coercive on $H_0^1(\Omega)$. Write $\rho_\Sigma(x)=\operatorname{dist}(x,\Sigma)$ and $2_s^*=\frac{2(N-s)}{N-2}$. If $H$ and $R_g$ denote respectively the norm of the mean curvature and the scalar curvature of $\Sigma$ (with its induced metric), which statement is the strongest valid existence assertion in this setting?

## Choices
A. There exist constants $A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ and $B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$, depending only on $N$, $\lambda$, $s_1$, and $s_2$, such that whenever there exists $y_0\in\Sigma$ with
\[
A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}H^2(y_0)+B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}R_g(y_0)+h(y_0)<0,
\]
there exists a nonzero nonnegative function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying
\[
-\Delta u(x)+h(x)u(x)=\lambda\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_2}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_2}}+\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_1}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_1}}
\quad\text{in }\Omega.
\]
B. There exist constants $A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ and $B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$, depending only on $N$, $\lambda$, $s_1$, and $s_2$, such that whenever there exists $y_0\in\Sigma$ with
\[
A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}H^2(y_0)+B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}R_g(y_0)+h(y_0)<0,
\]
there exists a nonzero nonnegative function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying
\[
-\Delta u(x)+h(x)u(x)=\lambda\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_1}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_1}}+\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_2}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_2}}
\quad\text{in }\Omega.
\]
C. There exist constants $A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ and $B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$, depending only on $N$, $\lambda$, $s_1$, and $s_2$, such that whenever there exists $y_0\in\Sigma$ with
\[
A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}H^2(y_0)+B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}R_g(y_0)+\frac{1}{\operatorname{Vol}_g(\Sigma)}\int_\Sigma h\,d\sigma_g<0,
\]
where $d\sigma_g$ is the induced volume measure on $\Sigma$, there exists a nonzero nonnegative function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying
\[
-\Delta u(x)+h(x)u(x)=\lambda\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_1}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_1}}+\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_2}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_2}}\quad\text{in }\Omega.
\]
D. If for some constants $A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ and $B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ depending only on $N$, $\lambda$, $s_1$, and $s_2$, one can find $y_0\in\Sigma$ such that
\[
A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}H^2(y_0)+B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}R_g(y_0)+h(y_0)<0,
\]
then there exists at least one nonzero nonnegative function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying
\[
-\Delta u(x)+h(x)u(x)=\lambda\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_1}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_1}}+\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_2}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_2}}
\quad\text{in }\Omega.
\]
E. There exist constants $A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$ and $B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}$, depending only on $N$, $\lambda$, $s_1$, and $s_2$, such that whenever there exists $y_0\in\Sigma$ with
\[
A_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}H^2(y_0)+B_{N,\lambda,s_1,s_2}R_g(y_0)+h(y_0)<0,
\]
there exists a nonzero nonnegative function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying
\[
-\Delta u(x)+h(x)u(x)=\lambda\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_1}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_1}}+\frac{u(x)^{2_{s_2}^*-1}}{\rho_\Sigma(x)^{s_2}}\quad\text{in }\Omega,
\]
and this solution has least energy among all nontrivial weak solutions of the same equation.
