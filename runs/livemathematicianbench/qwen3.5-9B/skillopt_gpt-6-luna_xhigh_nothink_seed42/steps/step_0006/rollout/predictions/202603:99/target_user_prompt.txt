## Question
Let \(k(n)\) denote the kissing number in \(\mathbb{R}^n\), i.e. the maximum number of nonoverlapping unit spheres that can simultaneously be tangent to a central unit sphere in \(n\)-dimensional Euclidean space. Which of the following is the strongest quantitative estimate that holds for \(k(19)\)?

## Choices
A. \(k(19) \ge 11948\).
B. \(k(19) \ge 13228\).
C. \(k(19) \ge 12204\).
D. \(k(19) \ge 11924\).
E. \(k(19) \ge 12268\).
