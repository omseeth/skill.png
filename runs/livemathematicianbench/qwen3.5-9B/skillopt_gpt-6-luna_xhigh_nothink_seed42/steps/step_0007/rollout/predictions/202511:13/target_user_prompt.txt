## Question
Let \(d_1,d_2,d_3>1\) be positive integers such that any repeated value among \(d_1,d_2,d_3\) is odd. Let
\[
R=F_{\mathbb{Q}}[x_1,x_2,x_3]/(x_1^2,x_2^2,x_3^2),\qquad |x_i|=d_i,
\]
where \(F_{\mathbb{Q}}[x_1,x_2,x_3]\) denotes the free graded-commutative \(\mathbb{Q}\)-algebra on homogeneous generators \(x_1,x_2,x_3\). Thus \(R\) is the cohomology algebra of a product of three positive-dimensional spheres over \(\mathbb{Q}\). If \(A\) is an order in \(R\), meaning that \(A\) is a graded subring of \(R\) whose underlying additive group is a graded lattice in \(R\) (equivalently, it is generated by a homogeneous basis of the \(\mathbb{Q}\)-vector space \(R\)), what is the strongest statement that can be proved about the existence of a CW-complex realizing \(A\) in integral cohomology?

## Choices
A. There exists a CW-complex \(X_A\) whose integral cohomology ring is isomorphic to \(A\); that is, \(H^*(X_A;\mathbb{Z})\cong A\).
B. There exists a CW-complex \(X_A\) whose integral cohomology ring is isomorphic to \(A\), provided that the degrees \(d_1,d_2,d_3\) are pairwise distinct.
C. There exists a single CW-complex \(X\), depending only on the degree list \((d_1,d_2,d_3)\) and not on the choice of order \(A\), such that for every order \(A\) in \(R\) one has \(H^*(X;\mathbb{Z})\cong A\).
D. There exists a CW-complex \(X_A\) whose integral cohomology ring is isomorphic to \(A\) after quotienting by torsion; that is, \(H^*(X_A;\mathbb{Z})/\mathrm{tors}\cong A\).
E. There exists a CW-complex \(X_A\) such that its integral cohomology ring embeds as a graded subring of \(A\); equivalently, \(H^*(X_A;\mathbb{Z})\cong B\) for some graded subring \(B\subseteq A\).
