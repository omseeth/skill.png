## Question
Let \(\zeta(k_1,\dots,k_r)=\sum_{n_1>\cdots>n_r\ge 1} n_1^{-k_1}\cdots n_r^{-k_r}\) denote the multiple zeta value, and let \(\{2\}^m\) mean a string of \(m\) copies of \(2\) (with \(\{2\}^0\) the empty string, which is omitted from the list of arguments). For every choice of positive integers \(a,b,c\), which identity is guaranteed to hold?

## Choices
A. \[\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},1,\{2\}^b)=\zeta(\{2\}^{a+b+c})+\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},3,\{2\}^{b-1})+\zeta(\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1}).\]
B. \[\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},1,\{2\}^b)=\zeta(\{2\}^{a+b+c})+\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1})+\zeta(\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},3,\{2\}^{a-1}).\]
C. \[\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},1,\{2\}^b)=\zeta(\{2\}^{a+b-1},4,\{2\}^{c-1})+\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1})+\zeta(\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1}).\]
D. \[\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},1,\{2\}^b)-\zeta(\{2\}^{a+b+c})-\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1})\] is a multiple zeta value of the form \[\zeta(\{2\}^{u-1},3,\{2\}^{v-1},3,\{2\}^{w-1})\] for some permutation \((u,v,w)\) of \((a,b,c)\).
E. \[\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1},1,\{2\}^b)=\zeta(\{2\}^{a+b+c})+\zeta(\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1})+\zeta(\{2\}^{b-1},3,\{2\}^{a-1},3,\{2\}^{c-1}).\]
