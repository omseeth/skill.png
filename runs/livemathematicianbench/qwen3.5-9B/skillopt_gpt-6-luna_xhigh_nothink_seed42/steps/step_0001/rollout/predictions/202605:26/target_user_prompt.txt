## Question
Let \(\Omega\subset \mathbb{R}^d\), with \(d\le 3\), be a smooth bounded open set, and let \(n(x)\) denote the outward unit normal on \(\partial\Omega\). For \(i=1,\dots,4\), fix diffusion coefficients \(d_i>0\) and nonnegative initial data \(a_i^{in}\in C^{\infty}(\overline{\Omega})\) satisfying the compatibility condition for homogeneous Neumann data, namely \(\nabla a_i^{in}\cdot n=0\) on \(\partial\Omega\). Consider the system for the unknowns \(a_i=a_i(t,x)\), \(i=1,\dots,4\),
\[
\partial_t a_i-d_i\Delta a_i = (-1)^i\,(a_1a_3-a_2a_4)
\quad \text{in } (0,\infty)\times\Omega,
\]
together with the boundary conditions \(\nabla a_i\cdot n=0\) on \((0,\infty)\times\partial\Omega\) and the initial conditions \(a_i(0,x)=a_i^{in}(x)\). Which statement is the strongest valid solvability claim in this setting?

## Choices
A. There exists at least one global smooth solution \((a_i)_{i=1}^4\) with each \(a_i\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_+\times\overline{\Omega})\), defined for all \(t\ge 0\), that satisfies the system, the homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions, and the prescribed initial data.
B. For such smooth nonnegative data, one can in general guarantee only a unique smooth solution on a time interval \([0,T]\), where \(T>0\) depends on the diffusion coefficients and the initial data; without an additional smallness assumption, global smooth solvability for all \(t\ge 0\) is not ensured.
C. There exists a unique smooth solution \((a_i)_{i=1}^4\) with each \(a_i\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_+\times\overline{\Omega})\), where \(\mathbb{R}_+=[0,\infty)\), defined for all \(t\ge 0\), that satisfies the system, the homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions, and the prescribed initial data.
D. There exists a unique smooth solution \((a_i)_{i=1}^4\) with each \(a_i\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_+\times\overline{\Omega})\), defined for all \(t\ge 0\), that satisfies the system, the homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions, and the prescribed initial data; moreover, the boundary compatibility condition \(\nabla a_i^{in}\cdot n=0\) on \(\partial\Omega\) is not actually needed for this global smooth solvability.
E. For every such compatible smooth datum, there exists a unique global classical solution \((a_i)_{i=1}^4\) on \(\mathbb{R}_+\times\overline{\Omega}\) satisfying the system and the homogeneous Neumann conditions, but the most one can conclude is \(a_i\in C^{\infty}((0,\infty)\times\overline{\Omega})\); smoothness on the closed time half-line up to \(t=0\) does not follow from the stated hypotheses.
