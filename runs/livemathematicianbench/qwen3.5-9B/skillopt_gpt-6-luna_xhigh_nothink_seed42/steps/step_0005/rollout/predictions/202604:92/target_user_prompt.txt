## Question
Let \(\Itre:=\{\, i(T)\mid T \text{ is a tree}\,\}\subseteq \mathbb N\), where \(i(T)\) denotes the number of independent sets of the tree \(T\) (an independent set is a set of vertices no two of which are adjacent). Define its lower growth exponent by
\[
\dimm(\Itre):=\liminf_{N\to\infty}\frac{\log |\Itre\cap\{1,\dots,N\}|}{\log N}.
\]
If \(\tau:=1+\sqrt2\), which quantitative lower bound holds for \(\dimm(\Itre)\)?

## Choices
A. \[\dimm(\Itre)\ge \frac{\log_{\tau}2}{4}\approx 0.1966.\]
B. There exists a constant \(c>0\) such that for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
|\Itre\cap\{1,\dots,N\}|\ge c\,N^{\frac{\log_{\tau}2}{4}}.
\]
C. \[\dimm(\Itre)>0.\]
D. \[\dimm(\Itre)\ge \frac{\log_{\tau}2}{3}\approx 0.2622.\]
E. \[\dimm(\Itre)\ge \frac{\log_{2}\tau}{4}\approx 0.3179.\]
