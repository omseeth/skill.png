## Question
Let \(H(y,p)=\tfrac12|p|^2+V(y)\) on \(\mathbb{R}^n\times\mathbb{R}^n\), where \(V\in \mathrm{Lip}(\mathbb{R}^n)\) is \(\mathbb{Z}^n\)-periodic, and let \(g\in W^{1,\infty}(\mathbb{R}^n)\). For each \(\varepsilon>0\), let \(u^\varepsilon\in C(\mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty))\) be the viscosity solution of
\[
\begin{cases}
 u_t^\varepsilon+\tfrac12|Du^\varepsilon|^2+V(x/\varepsilon)=\tfrac\varepsilon2\,\Delta u^\varepsilon & \text{in }\mathbb{R}^n\times(0,\infty),\\
 u^\varepsilon(x,0)=g(x) & \text{on }\mathbb{R}^n,
\end{cases}
\]
and let \(u\in C(\mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty))\) be the viscosity solution of the homogenized problem
\[
\begin{cases}
 u_t+\overline H(Du)=0 & \text{in }\mathbb{R}^n\times(0,\infty),\\
 u(x,0)=g(x) & \text{on }\mathbb{R}^n,
\end{cases}
\]
where \(\overline H\) is the effective Hamiltonian associated with \(H\). What is the strongest uniform quantitative estimate on \(|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\) that holds for all \((x,t)\in \mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty)\) and all \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\)?

## Choices
A. There exists a constant \(C=C\big(\|Dg\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},\|DV\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},n\big)>0\) such that, for every \((x,t)\in \mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty)\) and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\),
\[
|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\le C\,\varepsilon\bigl(1+\log(1+t/\varepsilon)\bigr).
\]
B. There exists a constant \(C=C\big(\|Dg\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},\|DV\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},n\big)>0\) such that, for every \((x,t)\in \mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty)\) and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\),
\[
|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\le \varepsilon\left(C+\frac n2\log\!\left(\frac{\max\{t,\varepsilon\}}{\varepsilon}\right)\right).
\]
C. There exists a constant \(C=C\big(\|Dg\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},\|DV\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},n\big)>0\) such that, for every \((x,t)\in \mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty)\) and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\),
\[
|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\le \varepsilon\left(C+\frac{n-1}{2}\log\!\left(\frac{\max\{t,\varepsilon\}}{\varepsilon}\right)\right).
\]
D. For each fixed \(t>0\), there exists a constant \(C_t=C_t\big(\|Dg\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},\|DV\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},n,t\big)>0\) such that, for every \(x\in\mathbb{R}^n\) and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\),
\[
|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\le C_t\,\varepsilon.
\]
E. There exists a constant \(C=C\big(\|Dg\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},\|DV\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)},n\big)>0\) such that, for every \((x,t)\in \mathbb{R}^n\times[0,\infty)\) and every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\),
\[
|u^\varepsilon(x,t)-u(x,t)|\le C\,\varepsilon|\log\varepsilon|.
\]
