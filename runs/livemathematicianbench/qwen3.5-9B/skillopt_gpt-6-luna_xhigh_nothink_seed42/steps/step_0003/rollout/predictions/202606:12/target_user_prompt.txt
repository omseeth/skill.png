## Question
Let
\[
H^{1/2}_+(\mathbb R):=\{f\in H^{1/2}(\mathbb R):\operatorname{supp}\widehat f\subset[0,\infty)\},
\]
and let $\Pi$ denote the Szeg\H{o} projector, i.e. the Fourier projector onto nonnegative frequencies. Let $u_0\in H^{1/2}_+(\mathbb R)$, and let $u(t,x)$ denote the solution of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation
\[
i\partial_t u=\Pi(|u|^2u),\qquad u(0,x)=u_0(x).
\]
Assume that the positive eigenvalues of $H_{u_0}^2$ are all simple, where $H_{u_0}$ is the Hankel operator associated with $u_0$. Which of the following is the strongest statement of the form "there exists ... such that for all ..." that holds under these assumptions?

## Choices
A. There exist a countable set $\mathcal V\subset (0,\infty)$, a family of positive integers $(N_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ with $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V} cN_c<\infty$, and a family $(R_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ of traveling quasi-periodic breather solutions of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation, where each $R_c$ has order $N_c$ and velocity $c$, such that for every $\eta>0$ there exists $\varepsilon>0$ with
\[
\limsup_{t\to\pm\infty}\left\|u(t)-\sum_{c\ge \varepsilon} R_c(t)\right\|_{H^{1/2}}<\eta.
\]
B. There exist a countable set $\mathcal V\subset (0,\infty)$, a family of positive integers $(N_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ with $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V} cN_c<\infty$, and a family $(R_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ of traveling quasi-periodic breather solutions of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation, where each $R_c$ has order $N_c$ and velocity $c$, such that
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^+}\,\lim_{t\to\pm\infty}\left\|u(t)-\sum_{c\ge \varepsilon} R_c(t)\right\|_{H^{1/2}}=0.
\]
C. There exist a countable set $\mathcal V\subset (0,\infty)$, a family of positive integers $(N_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ with $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V} cN_c<\infty$, a number $\delta>0$, and a family $(R_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ of traveling quasi-periodic breather solutions of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation, where each $R_c$ has order $N_c$ and velocity $c$, such that
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^+}\,\lim_{t\to\pm\infty}\left\|u(t)-\sum_{c\ge \varepsilon} R_c(t)\right\|_{H^{1/2+\delta}}=0.
\]
D. There exist a countable set $\mathcal V\subset (0,\infty)$, a family of positive integers $(N_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ with $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V} cN_c<\infty$, and a family $(R_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ of traveling quasi-periodic breather solutions of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation, where each $R_c$ has order $N_c$ and velocity $c$, such that the series $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V}R_c(t)$ converges in $H^{1/2}$ for each $t$, and
\[
\lim_{t\to\pm\infty}\left\|u(t)-\sum_{c\in \mathcal V} R_c(t)\right\|_{H^{1/2}}=0.
\]
E. There exist a countable set $\mathcal V\subset (0,\infty)$, a family of positive integers $(N_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ with $\sum_{c\in\mathcal V} N_c<\infty$, and a family $(R_c)_{c\in\mathcal V}$ of traveling quasi-periodic breather solutions of the cubic Szeg\H{o} equation, where each $R_c$ has order $N_c$ and velocity $c$, such that
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0^+}\,\lim_{t\to\pm\infty}\left\|u(t)-\sum_{c\ge \varepsilon} R_c(t)\right\|_{H^{1/2}}=0.
\]
