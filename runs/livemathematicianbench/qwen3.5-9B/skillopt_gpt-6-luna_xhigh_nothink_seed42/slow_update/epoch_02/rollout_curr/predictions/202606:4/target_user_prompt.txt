## Question
Let $G(n,p)$ be the Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi random graph on $n$ vertices, where each edge is included independently with probability $p$, and let $c(G)$ denote the cop number of a graph $G$ (the minimum number of cops needed to guarantee capture of the robber in the Cops and Robbers game on $G$). Fix $\alpha>0$ and assume $p=n^{\alpha-1+o(1)}$ as $n\to\infty$. Which of the following is the strongest valid asymptotic estimate for $c(G(n,p))$ that holds with high probability (that is, with probability tending to $1$ as $n\to\infty$)?

## Choices
A. With high probability, $c(G(n,p))=O(\sqrt{n}\,\log\log n)$.
B. There exists a constant $K=K(\alpha)>0$ such that with high probability $c(G(n,p))\sim K\sqrt{n}\,\log n$.
C. There exists an absolute constant $C>0$ such that for every fixed $\alpha>0$, whenever $p=n^{\alpha-1+o(1)}$, one has $\Pr\big(c(G(n,p))\le C\sqrt{n}\,\log n\big)\to 1$ as $n\to\infty$.
D. With high probability, $c(G(n,p))=O(\sqrt{n}\,\log n)$.
E. With high probability, $c(G(n,p))=n^{1/2+o(1)}$.
