## Question
Let $k\ge 2$ and $N\ge 1$ be integers, and let $f\in S_k(\Gamma_0(N))$ be a non-CM Hecke eigenform, where $S_k(\Gamma_0(N))$ denotes the space of cuspidal modular forms of weight $k$ and level $\Gamma_0(N)$. Write $K_f$ for the number field generated by the Hecke eigenvalues of $f$, and assume $K_f=\mathbb{Q}$. Suppose there exists an odd prime $p$ such that the local admissible representation $\pi_{f,p}$ attached to $f$ at $p$ is supercuspidal. Assume moreover that, if the restriction of the local $p$-adic Galois representation $\rho_{f,p}$ to a decomposition group $G_p$ at $p$ is induced, then $p\ge 5$ and the image of its mod-$p$ reduction $\overline{\rho}_{f,p}$ contains $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)$. For the associated adelic Galois representation $\rho_f:G_{\mathbb{Q}}\to \mathrm{GL}_2(\widehat{\mathbb{Z}})$, where $G_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is the absolute Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ is the profinite completion of $\mathbb{Z}$, which of the following is the strongest conclusion that follows?

## Choices
A. The adelic representation \rho_f has property \(\mathrm{B}\).
B. The adelic representation \rho_f has property \(\mathrm{SB}\) as soon as there is an odd prime \(p\) for which \(\pi_{f,p}\) is in the discrete series, with the same induced-case residual-image condition.
C. The adelic representation \rho_f has property \(\mathrm{SB}\) even if, in the induced case, one drops the condition \(p\ge 5\) and assumes only that the image of \(\overline{\rho}_{f,p}\) contains \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)\).
D. The adelic representation \rho_f has property SB.
E. The adelic representation \rho_f has property \(\mathrm{SB}\) if, in the induced case, the large residual-image condition is satisfied for some prime \(\ell\ge 5\), not necessarily for the same prime \(p\) at which \(\pi_{f,p}\) is supercuspidal.
