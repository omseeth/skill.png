## Question
Let $M$ be a geodesically complete Riemannian manifold of dimension $n$ with Riemannian measure $\mu$. Assume $p>1$, $q>0$, and set $D=1-q(p-1)$. Assume also that $p>nD$ and $n>p$. Which of the following statements is equivalent to the Sobolev inequality
\[
\left(\int_M |v|^{\frac{pn}{n-p}}\,d\mu\right)^{\frac{n-p}{n}}\le C\int_M |\nabla v|^p\,d\mu\qquad\text{for all }v\in W^{1,p}(M)?
\]

## Choices
A. For every nonnegative bounded solution $u$ of
\[
\partial_t u=\Delta_p(u^q):=\operatorname{div}\big(|\nabla(u^q)|^{p-2}\nabla(u^q)\big)
\]
on $M\times\mathbb{R}_+$ with initial datum $u(\cdot,0)=u_0\in L^1(M)\cap L^\infty(M)$, there exists a constant $C=C(p,q,n)>0$ such that for every $t>0$,
\[
\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}\le C\,\|u_0\|_{L^1(M)}^{\frac{p}{p-nD}}\,t^{-\frac{n}{p-nD}}.
\]
B. For every nonnegative bounded solution $u$ of
\[
\partial_t u=\Delta_p(u^q):=\operatorname{div}\big(|\nabla(u^q)|^{p-2}\nabla(u^q)\big)
\]
on $M\times\mathbb{R}_+$ with initial datum $u(\cdot,0)=u_0\in L^1(M)\cap L^\infty(M)$, there exists a constant $C=C(p,q,n)>0$ such that for every $t>0$,
\[
\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}\le C\,\|u_0\|_{L^1(M)}^{\frac{p}{p-nD}}\,t^{-\frac{n}{p-(n-1)D}}.
\]
C. For every nonnegative bounded solution $u$ of
\[
\partial_t u=\Delta_p(u^q):=\operatorname{div}\big(|\nabla(u^q)|^{p-2}\nabla(u^q)\big)
\]
on $M\times\mathbb{R}_+$ with initial datum $u(\cdot,0)=u_0\in L^1(M)\cap L^\infty(M)$, there exists a constant $C=C(p,q,n)>0$ such that for every $t>0$,
\[
\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}\le C\,\|u_0\|_{L^1(M)}^{\frac{p}{p-D}}\,t^{-\frac{n}{p-nD}}.
\]
D. For every $a>1$, there exists a constant $C=C(p,q,n,a)>0$ such that every nonnegative bounded solution $u$ of
\[
\partial_t u=\Delta_p(u^q):=\operatorname{div}\big(|\nabla(u^q)|^{p-2}\nabla(u^q)\big)
\]
on $M\times\mathbb{R}_+$ with initial datum $u(\cdot,0)=u_0\in L^a(M)\cap L^\infty(M)$ satisfies, for every $t>0$,
\[
\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}\le C\,\|u_0\|_{L^a(M)}^{\frac{ap}{ap-nD}}\,t^{-\frac{n}{p-nD}}.
\]
E. For every nonnegative bounded solution $u$ of
\[
\partial_t u=\Delta_p(u^q):=\operatorname{div}\big(|\nabla(u^q)|^{p-2}\nabla(u^q)\big)
\]
on $M\times\mathbb{R}_+$ with initial datum $u(\cdot,0)=u_0\in L^1(M)\cap L^\infty(M)$, there exists a constant $C>0$ such that for every $t>0$,
\[
\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty(M)}\le C\,\|u_0\|_{L^1(M)}^{\frac{p}{p-nD}}\,t^{-\frac{n}{p-nD}}.
