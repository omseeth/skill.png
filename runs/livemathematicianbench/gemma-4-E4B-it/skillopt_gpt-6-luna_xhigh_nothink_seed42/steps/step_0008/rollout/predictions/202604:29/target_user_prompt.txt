## Question
Let \(n\ge 2\), let \(F_n\) denote a free group of rank \(n\), and let \(F_2\) denote a free group of rank \(2\). For homomorphisms \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\), define their equalizer by
\[
\operatorname{Eq}(g,h)=\{x\in F_n: g(x)=h(x)\},
\]
and write \(\operatorname{rk}(G)\) for the rank of a free group \(G\). (Here, a monomorphism means an injective homomorphism.) Which of the following is the strongest valid existence statement in this setting?

## Choices
A. There exist monomorphisms \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\) such that \(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h)\) is a free factor of \(F_n\) and \(\operatorname{rk}(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h))\ge 2n-2\).
B. For every pair of monomorphisms \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\), one has \(\operatorname{rk}(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h))\le n\).
C. There exist monomorphisms \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\) such that \(\operatorname{rk}(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h))\ge 2n-2\).
D. There exist monomorphisms \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\) such that \(\operatorname{rk}(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h))\ge n\).
E. Whenever at least one of \(g,h:F_n\to F_2\) is a monomorphism, one must have \(\operatorname{rk}(\operatorname{Eq}(g,h))\le n\).
