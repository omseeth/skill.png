## Question
Let $K$ be a global function field of characteristic $p>0$, let $K^p\subseteq K$ denote the subfield of $p$th powers, and write $\mathbf A_K$ for the ring of adeles of $K$. Let $(X,\mathcal A)$ be a smooth projective variety with an ample line bundle over $K$, and assume that $(X,\mathcal A)$ descends to $(\widetilde X,\widetilde{\mathcal A})$ over $K^p$. Suppose either
(a) $H^1(X,\mathcal A^{-1})\neq 0$ and $\mathcal A^{\otimes p^{n(\mathcal A)}}$ is globally generated, or
(b) $H^0(X,B_X^1\otimes \mathcal A^{-1})\neq 0$, where $B_X^1$ is the sheaf of locally exact differentials on $X$, and $\mathcal A^{\otimes p}$ is globally generated.
Set $m=p^{n(\mathcal A)}$ in case (a) and $m=p$ in case (b). Let $Y$ be the quotient of the corresponding foliation $\ker(\widetilde{\mathcal A}^{\otimes m})$ on $\widetilde X$, let $\phi:Y\to X$ be the resulting purely inseparable morphism, and let $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}:X\to \mathbb P^N_K$ be the morphism induced by $\mathcal A^{\otimes m}$. If $(x_v)\in X(\mathbf A_K)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}$ does not lie in the image of $Y(\mathbf A_K)\to X(\mathbf A_K)$, where $X(\mathbf A_K)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}$ denotes the adelic points orthogonal to the $p$-torsion subgroup of $\mathrm{Br}(X)$, which strongest conclusion follows?

## Choices
A. Because $\phi:Y\to X$ is purely inseparable, any $(x_v)\in X(\mathbf A_K)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}$ that does not lie in the image of $Y(\mathbf A_K)\to X(\mathbf A_K)$ must already belong to $X(K)$; equivalently,
\[
\bigl(X(\mathbf A_K)\setminus \phi(Y(\mathbf A_K))\bigr)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}=X(K)\setminus \phi(Y(K)).
\]
B. One has $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}((x_v))\in \mathbb P^N_K(K)$. Moreover, if $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}$ is generically injective onto its image, then $(x_v)\in X(K)$, and hence
\[
\bigl(X(\mathbf A_K)\setminus \phi(Y(\mathbf A_K))\bigr)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}=X(K)\setminus \phi(Y(K)).
\]
C. One has $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}((x_v))\in \mathbb P^N_K(K)$. Moreover, if $\mathcal A^{\otimes m}$ is globally generated and the induced morphism $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}:X\to \mathbb P^N_K$ is finite onto its image, then $(x_v)\in X(K)$, and hence
\[
\bigl(X(\mathbf A_K)\setminus \phi(Y(\mathbf A_K))\bigr)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}=X(K)\setminus \phi(Y(K)).
\]
D. One has $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}((x_v))\in \mathbb P^N_K(K)$. Moreover, if $\mathcal A^{\otimes m}$ separates points, then $(x_v)\in X(K)$, and hence
\[
\bigl(X(\mathbf A_K)\setminus \phi(Y(\mathbf A_K))\bigr)^{\mathrm{Br}(X)[p]}=X(K)\setminus \phi(Y(K)).
\]
E. One has $\psi_{\mathcal A^{\otimes m}}((x_v))\in \mathbb P^N_K(K)$.
