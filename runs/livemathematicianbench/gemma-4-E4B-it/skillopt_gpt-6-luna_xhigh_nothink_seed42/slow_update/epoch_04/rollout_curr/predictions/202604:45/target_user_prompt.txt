## Question
Let \(R>0\) be fixed, let \(\mathbb T=\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)\), let \(Q_z^{\theta}\) denote rotation by angle \(\theta\) about the \(x_3\)-axis, and let \(\mathbf e_3=(0,0,1)\). For \(k\in\mathbb N\) with \(k\ge 2\), define the critical angular velocity by \(\Omega_k=\sqrt{k^2-1}/R^2\). An axial screw motion is a map of the form
\[
\mathbf x(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega t}\,\mathbf y(s+ct)+Vt\mathbf e_3,
\qquad (s,t)\in \mathbb T\times[0,\infty),
\]
with \((\Omega,\mathbf y,c,V)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R\), where \(H^4(\mathbb T)\) is the Sobolev space of \(2\pi\)-periodic functions with four weak derivatives in \(L^2\). Let \(\mathbf x_0^R(s)=(R\cos s,R\sin s,0)\) be the reference radius-\(R\) circle. Which statement gives the strongest existence result in this setting?

## Choices
A. There exists \(\varepsilon_k>0\) and a smooth map
\[
(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\ni \lambda\mapsto (\Omega^\lambda,\mathbf y^\lambda,c^\lambda,V^\lambda)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R
\]
such that
\[
(\Omega^0,\mathbf y^0,c^0,V^0)=(\Omega_k,\mathbf x_0^R,-R\Omega_k,1/R),
\]
and for every \(\lambda\in(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\), the formula
\[
\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega^\lambda t}\,\mathbf y^\lambda(s+c^\lambda t)+V^\lambda t\mathbf e_3
\]
defines an axial screw motion. In addition, as \(\lambda\to 0\),
\[
V^\lambda=
\frac{1}{R}
+\left(\frac{k^2(k^2-1)}{2R^3}\right)\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2).
\]
B. There exists \(\varepsilon_k>0\) and a smooth map
\[
(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\ni \lambda\mapsto (\Omega^\lambda,\mathbf y^\lambda,c^\lambda,V^\lambda)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R
\]
such that
\[
(\Omega^0,\mathbf y^0,c^0,V^0)=(\Omega_k,\mathbf x_0^R,-R\Omega_k,1/R),
\]
and for every \(\lambda\in(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\), the formula
\[
\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega^\lambda t}\,\mathbf y^\lambda(s+c^\lambda t)+V^\lambda t\mathbf e_3
\]
defines an axial screw motion.
C. There exists \(\varepsilon_k>0\) and a smooth map
\[
(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\ni \lambda\mapsto (\Omega^\lambda,\mathbf y^\lambda,c^\lambda,V^\lambda)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R
\]
such that
\[
(\Omega^0,\mathbf y^0,c^0,V^0)=(\Omega_k,\mathbf x_0^R,-R\Omega_k,1/R),
\]
and for every \(\lambda\in(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\), the formula
\[
\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega^\lambda t}\,\mathbf y^\lambda(s+c^\lambda t)+V^\lambda t\mathbf e_3
\]
defines an axial screw motion. Moreover, for every nonzero \(\lambda\), there exist constants \(C_\lambda,t_{0,\lambda}>0\) such that
\[
\bigl|\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)-\bigl(Q_z^{\Omega_k t}\mathbf x_0^R(s-R\Omega_k t)+(t/R)\mathbf e_3\bigr)\bigr|\le C_\lambda
\]
for all \((s,t)\in \mathbb T\times[t_{0,\lambda},\infty)\).
D. There exists \(\varepsilon_k>0\) and a smooth map
\[
(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\ni \lambda\mapsto (\Omega^\lambda,\mathbf y^\lambda,c^\lambda,V^\lambda)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R
\]
such that
\[
(\Omega^0,\mathbf y^0,c^0,V^0)=(\Omega_k,\mathbf x_0^R,-R\Omega_k,1/R),
\]
and for every \(\lambda\in(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\), the formula
\[
\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega^\lambda t}\,\mathbf y^\lambda(s+c^\lambda t)+V^\lambda t\mathbf e_3
\]
defines an axial screw motion; moreover, for every sufficiently small nonzero \(\lambda\), one has \(V^\lambda>1/R\).
E. There exists \(\varepsilon_k>0\) and a map
\[
(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\ni \lambda\mapsto (\Omega^\lambda,\mathbf y^\lambda,c^\lambda,V^\lambda)\in \mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb T)^3\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R
\]
such that
\[
(\Omega^0,\mathbf y^0,c^0,V^0)=(\Omega_k,\mathbf x_0^R,-R\Omega_k,1/R),
\]
and for every \(\lambda\in(-\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k)\), the formula
\[
\mathbf x^{(\lambda)}(s,t)=Q_z^{\Omega^\lambda t}\,\mathbf y^\lambda(s+c^\lambda t)+V^\lambda t\mathbf e_3
\]
defines an axial screw motion.
