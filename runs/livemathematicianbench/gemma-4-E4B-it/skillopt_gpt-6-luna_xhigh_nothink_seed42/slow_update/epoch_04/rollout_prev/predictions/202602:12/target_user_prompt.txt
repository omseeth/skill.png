## Question
For integers \(n\ge 1\) and \(0\le m<n\), write \(H(n,m)^{\mathrm{ab}}\) for the abelianization of the group \(H(n,m)\). Which of the following statements is equivalent to \(H(n,m)^{\mathrm{ab}}\cong \mathbb{Z}^2\)?

## Choices
A. \(n\equiv 0\pmod 6\) and \(m\equiv 2\pm \frac n3\pmod n\).
B. \(n\equiv 0\pmod 6\) and \(m\equiv 2\pmod{n/3}\).
C. \(n\equiv 0\pmod 6\) and \(m\equiv 2\pmod n\).
D. \(n\equiv 0\pmod 6\) and \(m\equiv 2+\frac n2\pmod n\).
E. \(n\equiv 0\pmod 6\) and \(m=2\).
