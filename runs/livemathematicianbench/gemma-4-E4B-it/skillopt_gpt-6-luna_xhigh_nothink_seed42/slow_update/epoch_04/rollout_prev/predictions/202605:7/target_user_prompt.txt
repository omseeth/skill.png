## Question
Consider the Cauchy problem
\[
 u_t(t,x)+\left(\frac{u^2(t,x)}{2}\right)_x={\bf N}[u(t,\cdot)](x),
 \qquad u(0,\cdot)=\bar u,
\]
on \([0,\infty)\times \mathbb{R}\), where \({\bf N}:H^1(\mathbb{R})\to L^2(\mathbb{R})\) is a bounded linear operator acting on the spatial profile \(u(t,\cdot)\). What is the strongest correct statement characterizing when this problem exhibits wave breaking (shock formation) at a time \(T^*>0\)?

## Choices
A. It exhibits wave breaking at time \(T^*>0\) precisely if the Cauchy problem admits a unique classical solution \(u\) on \([0,T^*)\times\mathbb{R}\), extends continuously to \(t=T^*\) as an \(H^1(\mathbb{R})\)-valued map, and satisfies
\[
\limsup_{t\to T^{*-}}\left(\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}} u_x(t,x)\right)=-\infty.
\]
B. It exhibits wave breaking at time \(T^*>0\) precisely if the Cauchy problem admits a unique classical solution \(u\) on \([0,T^*)\times\mathbb{R}\) such that
\[
\liminf_{t\to T^{*-}}\left(\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}} u_x(t,x)\right)=-\infty.
\]
C. If the Cauchy problem admits a unique classical solution \(u\) on \([0,T^*)\times\mathbb{R}\) such that
\[
\limsup_{t\to T^{*-}}\left(\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}} u_x(t,x)\right)=-\infty,
\]
then it exhibits wave breaking at time \(T^*>0\).
D. It exhibits wave breaking at time \(T^*>0\) precisely if the Cauchy problem admits a unique classical solution \(u\) on \([0,T^*)\times\mathbb{R}\) such that
\[
\limsup_{t\to T^{*-}} \|u_x(t,\cdot)\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R})}=+\infty.
\]
E. It exhibits wave breaking at time \(T^*>0\) precisely if the Cauchy problem admits a unique classical solution \(u\) on \([0,T^*)\times\mathbb{R}\) such that
\[
\limsup_{t\to T^{*-}}\left(\inf_{x\in\mathbb{R}} u_x(t,x)\right)=-\infty.
\]
