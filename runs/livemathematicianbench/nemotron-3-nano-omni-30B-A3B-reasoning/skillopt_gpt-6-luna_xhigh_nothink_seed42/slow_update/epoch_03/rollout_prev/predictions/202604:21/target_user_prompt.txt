## Question
Let $L(\lambda)$ be a finite-dimensional irreducible representation of $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ with highest weight $\lambda=m_1\omega_1+2m_2\omega_2$, where $m_1,m_2\in\mathbb Z_{\ge 0}$ and $\omega_1,\omega_2$ are the fundamental weights; when referring to shape, view $\lambda$ as the partition $(m_1+m_2,m_2)$. Let $v_\lambda$ be a highest weight vector, and let $f_1,f_2$ be the root vectors corresponding to the negative simple roots. Define the set of Verma vectors
\[
H:= \left\{ f_1^{b_4} f_2^{b_3} f_1^{b_2} f_2^{b_1} v_\lambda \,\middle|\,
\begin{aligned}
&b_i\in \mathbb Z_{\ge 0}\ (i=1,2,3,4),\\
&0\le b_1\le 2m_2,\\
&0\le b_2\le m_1+b_1,\\
&0\le b_3\le \min\{b_2+m_1,\,2b_2\},\\
&0\le b_4\le \min\{m_1,\,\tfrac12 b_3\}
\end{aligned}
\right\}.
\]
Which objects are in weight-preserving one-to-one correspondence with the elements of $H$?

## Choices
A. The Kashiwara--Nakashima tableaux for $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ of shape $\lambda$, except that $0$ is also required to be strictly increasing down each column.
B. The tableaux of shape $\lambda$ for $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ that satisfy the Kashiwara--Nakashima row and column conditions and never place $1$ and $\overline{1}$ in the same column, but do not impose the adjacent-column exclusion involving $0$ next to a column containing $\overline{2}$.
C. A weight-preserving subset of the Kashiwara--Nakashima tableaux for $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ of shape $\lambda$.
D. The tableaux of shape $\lambda$ for $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ that satisfy the Kashiwara--Nakashima row and column conditions and never place $1$ and $\overline{1}$ in the same column, but do not impose the adjacent-column exclusion involving $2$ and $\overline{2}$.
E. The Kashiwara--Nakashima tableaux for $\mathfrak{spo}(4|1)$ of shape $\lambda$.
