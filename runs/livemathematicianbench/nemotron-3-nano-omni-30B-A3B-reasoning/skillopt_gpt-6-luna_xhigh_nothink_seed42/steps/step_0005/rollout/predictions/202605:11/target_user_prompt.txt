## Question
Let $M$ be a cancellative commutative monoid, and let FFM mean finite factorization monoid. A submonoid $N\subseteq M$ is called an undermonoid if its Grothendieck group agrees with that of $M$, i.e. $\mathcal G(N)=\mathcal G(M)$ (viewing $\mathcal G(N)$ as the subgroup of $\mathcal G(M)$ generated by $N$). Which statement is equivalent to the assertion that every submonoid of $M$ is an FFM?

## Choices
A. Every submonoid $N\subseteq M$ for which $\mathcal G(M)/\mathcal G(N)$ is finite is an FFM.
B. Every undermonoid of $M$ is an FFM.
C. Every undermonoid $N\subseteq M$ satisfying $\mathcal U(N)=\mathcal U(M)\cap N$ is an FFM.
D. Every undermonoid of $M$ is a BFM.
E. Every submonoid $N\subseteq M$ satisfying $\mathcal G(N)=\mathcal G(M)$ and $\mathcal G(N)\cap M=N$ is an FFM.
