## Question
Suppose \(p\) is an \((m,n)\) Dyck path, and let \(a(p)\) denote the number of anchor points of \(p\) away from the origin, where an anchor is a vertex of \(p\) whose coordinates lie on the diagonal \(ny-mx=0\). If \(w_P(p)\) denotes the anchored weight of \(p\), which exact statement holds for every such \(p\)?

## Choices
A. The anchored weight is given exactly by
\[
w_P(p)=\frac{m+n}{a(p)},
\]
and therefore \(w_P(p)\in \mathbb{Q}\).
B. The anchored weight is given exactly by
\[
w_P(p)=\frac{\gcd(m,n)}{a(p)},
\]
and therefore \(w_P(p)\in \mathbb{Q}\).
C. The anchored weight is given exactly by
\[
w_P(p)=\frac{\gcd(m,n)}{a(p)+1},
\]
where \(a(p)+1\) is the total number of anchors including the origin, and therefore \(w_P(p)\in \mathbb{Q}\).
D. The anchored weight is given exactly by
\[
w_P(p)=\frac{1}{a(p)},
\]
so the value depends only on the number of anchor points away from the origin and not on \(\gcd(m,n)\).
E. The anchored weight of every \((m,n)\) Dyck path is a rational number; in particular,
\[
w_P(p)\in\mathbb{Q}.
\]
