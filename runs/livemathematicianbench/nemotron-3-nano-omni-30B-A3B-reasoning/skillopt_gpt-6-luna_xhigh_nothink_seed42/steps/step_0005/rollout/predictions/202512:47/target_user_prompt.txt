## Question
Let \((B_t)_{t\ge 0}\) be a planar Brownian motion started at \(0\), let \(\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}\) and \(\mathbb S^1=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}\), and let \(\tau_{\mathbb D}\) be the first hitting time of \(\mathbb S^1\). For \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\), let \(\mathcal B_\varepsilon\) be the event that there exist two disjoint subpaths of the trajectory \(B[0,\tau_{\mathbb D}]\) joining \(\varepsilon\mathbb S^1\) and \(\tfrac12\mathbb S^1\). What is the strongest statement that can be proved about the probabilities \(\mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\) uniformly for all \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\)?

## Choices
A. There exist constants \(C_1,C_2>0\) such that for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\),
\[
\frac{C_1}{(\log|\log\varepsilon|)^{1/2}}\le \mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\le \frac{C_2}{\log|\log\varepsilon|}.
\]
B. There exist constants \(C_1,C_2>0\) such that for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\),
\[
\frac{C_1}{|\log\varepsilon|}\le \mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\le \frac{C_2}{|\log\varepsilon|}.
\]
C. There exist constants \(C_1,C_2>0\) such that for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\),
\[
\frac{C_1}{\log|\log\varepsilon|}\le \mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\le \frac{C_2}{\log|\log\varepsilon|}.
\]
D. There exists a constant \(C_2>0\) such that for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\),
\[
\mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\le \frac{C_2}{\log|\log\varepsilon|}.
\]
E. There exist constants \(C_1,C_2>0\) such that for every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/10)\),
\[
\frac{C_1}{\log|\log\varepsilon|}\le \mathbb P[\mathcal B_\varepsilon]\le \frac{C_2}{(\log|\log\varepsilon|)^{3/2}}.
\]
