## Question
Fix an integer \(n\ge 3\). Let \(\mathbb B^n\subset \mathbb R^n\) be the unit ball, let \(\mathrm{Sym}_n\) denote the real symmetric \(n\times n\) matrices, and write \(L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\) for essentially bounded measurable \(\mathrm{Sym}_n\)-valued matrix fields. A function \(u\) is positively \(\alpha\)-homogeneous if \(u(tx)=t^\alpha u(x)\) whenever \(t>0\) and \(x,tx\in \mathbb B^n\). Which of the following is the strongest valid statement asserting the existence of uniformly elliptic measurable coefficients and homogeneous weak solutions of a divergence-form equation in \(\mathbb B^n\), with explicit dependence on a parameter \(\gamma\ge 1\)?

## Choices
A. There exist constants \(C_n,c_n>0\) and a single measurable symmetric matrix field \(A\in L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\) such that, for every \(\gamma\ge 1\), there are a positively \(\alpha_\gamma\)-homogeneous function \(u_\gamma\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb B^n)\) with \(\operatorname{div}(A\nabla u_\gamma)=0\) weakly in \(\mathbb B^n\), the fixed ellipticity bounds \(\frac1{C_n}|\xi|^2\le A(x)\xi\cdot\xi\le C_n|\xi|^2\) hold for almost every \(x\in\mathbb B^n\) and every \(\xi\in\mathbb R^n\), and \(\alpha_\gamma\le e^{-c_n\gamma}\).
B. There exist constants \(C_n,c_n>0\) such that for every \(\gamma\ge 1\) there are a measurable symmetric matrix field \(A_\gamma\in L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\), an ellipticity constant \(K_\gamma=C_n\gamma\), and a positively \(\alpha_\gamma\)-homogeneous function \(u_\gamma\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb B^n)\) for which \(\operatorname{div}(A_\gamma\nabla u_\gamma)=0\) weakly in \(\mathbb B^n\), the ellipticity bounds \(\frac1{K_\gamma}|\xi|^2\le A_\gamma(x)\xi\cdot\xi\le K_\gamma|\xi|^2\) hold for almost every \(x\in\mathbb B^n\) and every \(\xi\in\mathbb R^n\), and the homogeneity exponent satisfies the two-sided estimate \(c_n e^{-C_n K_\gamma}\le \alpha_\gamma\le C_n e^{-c_n K_\gamma}\).
C. There exists a constant \(C_n>0\) such that for every \(\gamma\ge 1\) there are a measurable symmetric matrix field \(A_\gamma\in L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\), an ellipticity constant \(K_\gamma\le C_n\gamma\), and a positively \(\alpha_\gamma\)-homogeneous function \(u_\gamma\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb B^n)\) for which \(\operatorname{div}(A_\gamma\nabla u_\gamma)=0\) weakly in \(\mathbb B^n\), and \(\frac1{K_\gamma}|\xi|^2\le A_\gamma(x)\xi\cdot\xi\le K_\gamma|\xi|^2\) holds for almost every \(x\in\mathbb B^n\) and every \(\xi\in\mathbb R^n\).
D. There exist constants \(C_n,c_n>0\) such that for every \(\gamma\ge 1\) there are a measurable symmetric matrix field \(A_\gamma\in L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\), an ellipticity constant \(K_\gamma=C_n\gamma\), and a positively \(\alpha_\gamma\)-homogeneous function \(u_\gamma\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb B^n)\) for which \(\operatorname{div}(A_\gamma \nabla u_\gamma)=0\) weakly in \(\mathbb B^n\), the ellipticity bounds \(\frac1{K_\gamma}|\xi|^2\le A_\gamma(x)\xi\cdot\xi\le K_\gamma|\xi|^2\) hold for almost every \(x\in\mathbb B^n\) and every \(\xi\in\mathbb R^n\), and the homogeneity exponent satisfies \(\alpha_\gamma\le e^{-c_n K_\gamma}\).
E. There exist constants \(C_n,c_n>0\) such that for every \(\gamma\ge 1\) there are a measurable symmetric matrix field \(A_\gamma\in L^\infty(\mathbb B^n,\mathrm{Sym}_n)\), an ellipticity constant \(K_\gamma\le C_n\), and a positively \(\alpha_\gamma\)-homogeneous function \(u_\gamma\in W^{1,2}(\mathbb B^n)\) for which \(\operatorname{div}(A_\gamma\nabla u_\gamma)=0\) weakly in \(\mathbb B^n\), the ellipticity bounds \(\frac1{K_\gamma}|\xi|^2\le A_\gamma(x)\xi\cdot\xi\le K_\gamma|\xi|^2\) hold for almost every \(x\in\mathbb B^n\) and every \(\xi\in\mathbb R^n\), and the homogeneity exponent satisfies \(\alpha_\gamma\le e^{-c_n\gamma}\).
