## Question
Let $\ell$ be an odd prime, let $N\ge 1$, let $\delta\ge 1$ be an integer that is $\ell^N$-th-power-free, and let $r,s,t>0$ satisfy $r+s+t=\ell^N$. Let $\phi_{\delta}^{(N)}$ denote the CM abelian variety under consideration. Let $\epsilon'_N\in\mu_{\ell-1}(\mathbf{Q}_{\ell})$ (the group of $(\ell-1)$st roots of unity in $\mathbf{Q}_{\ell}$), $b'_N\in\mathbf{Z}$, and $c'_N\in \ell\mathbf{Z}_{\ell}$ satisfy
$$r^r s^s (\ell^N-t)^t\delta^{r+s}=\epsilon'_N\ell^{b'_N}(1+c'_N),$$
and put
$$w=\min\{\operatorname{ord}_{\ell}(b'_N),\operatorname{ord}_{\ell}(c'_N)\},$$
where $\operatorname{ord}_{\ell}$ is the $\ell$-adic valuation. Which description gives the complete factor-by-factor classification of the global root number $W(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})=\prod_{p\le \infty}W_p(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})$ into local terms $W_p(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})$, with the product taken over all finite primes $p$ and the archimedean place $p=\infty$?

## Choices
A. $$W(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})=\prod_{p\le \infty}W_p(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)}),$$ where for $p\neq \ell$,
$$W_p(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})=\begin{cases}
 i^{-\frac{\ell^{N-1}(\ell-1)}{2}} & (p=\infty),\\[2mm]
 \left(\frac{p}{\ell}\right) & (p\mid \delta),\\[2mm]
 1 & (p\nmid \delta),
\end{cases}$$
and
$$W_{\ell}(\phi_{\delta}^{(N)})=\begin{cases}
-\left(\frac{\pm r'st\,\frac{c'_N}{\ell^N}J(N,2\ell^{N-w})}{\ell}\right)i^{\frac{\ell^{N-1}(\ell-1)}{2}} & \bigl(0\le w\le N,\ \operatorname{ord}_{\ell}(b'_N+c'_N)=w\bigr),\\[2mm]
-\left(\frac{r'st\,\frac{c'_N e}{\ell^N}J(N,\ell^{N-w-1}(\ell-1))}{\ell}\right)i^{\frac{\ell^{N-1}(\ell-1)}{2}} & \bigl(1\le w<N,\ \operatorname{ord}_{\ell}(b'_N+c'_N)>w\bigr),\\[2mm]
\left(\frac{2}{\ell}\right)i^{\frac{\ell^{N-1}(\ell-1)}{2}} & (\text{otherwise}).
\end{cases}$$
Here $\left(\frac{\cdot}{\ell}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol modulo $\ell$, $r=\ell^{\operatorname{ord}_{\ell}(r)}r'$ with $\ell\nmid r'$, $\pm=-1$ if and only if $w=N$, and
$$e=\begin{cases}2 & (w=N-1),\\ 1 & (\text{otherwise}).\end{cases}$$
