## Question
Let \(\mathcal C\) be a block-stable class of connected graphs, and let \(\mathcal B\) be the class of its 2-connected components (blocks). Write \(B(y)\) for the exponential generating function of \(\mathcal B\), let \(\rho_B\) be the radius of convergence of \(B\), and define
\[
u_C:=\frac{1}{\rho_B B''(\rho_B)}.
\]
Let \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) be the exponential generating function of connected rooted graphs in \(\mathcal C\), where \(x\) marks vertices and \(u\) marks blocks. For fixed \(u>0\), let \(\mathbb P_\nu\) denote the fixed-size block-weighted law under which a rooted graph \(G\in\mathcal C^{\bullet}\) with \(n\) vertices is sampled with probability proportional to \(u^{b(G)}\), where \(b(G)\) is the number of blocks of \(G\). The block tree of a sampled graph is the tree recording how its blocks are attached. Which statement holds for every such class \(\mathcal C\)?

## Choices
A. The behaviour changes at the threshold \(u_C=1/(\rho_B B''(\rho_B))\): the singular behaviour of \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) depends on whether \(u<u_C\) or not, and the block tree of a graph sampled according to \(\mathbb P_\nu\) has a subcritical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law when \(u<u_C\), while it has a critical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law when \(u\ge u_C\).
B. The behaviour changes at the threshold \(u_C=1/(\rho_B B''(\rho_B))\): the singular behaviour of \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) depends on whether \(u<u_C\) or not, and the block tree of a graph sampled according to \(\mathbb P_\nu\) has a subcritical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law when \(u<u_C\), a critical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law only at \(u=u_C\), and a supercritical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law when \(u>u_C\).
C. The behaviour changes at the threshold \(u_C=1/(\rho_B B''(\rho_B))\): the singular behaviour of \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) depends on whether \(u<u_C\) or not, but after conditioning on the size \(n\) the block tree of a graph sampled according to \(\mathbb P_\nu\) always has a critical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law, for every \(u>0\).
D. The behaviour changes at the threshold \(u_C=1/(\rho_B B''(\rho_B))\): in particular, the singular behaviour of \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) depends on whether \(u<u_C\) or not.
E. The behaviour changes at the threshold \(u_C=1/(\rho_B B''(\rho_B))\): the singular behaviour of \(C^{\bullet}(x,u)\) depends on whether \(u<u_C\) or not, and under \(\mathbb P_\nu\) it is the decorated block tree itself that has a subcritical Bienaymé–Galton–Watson law when \(u<u_C\) and a critical one when \(u\ge u_C\), whereas the underlying undecorated block tree need not be Bienaymé–Galton–Watson.
