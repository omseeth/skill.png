## Question
Let \(\Sigma\) be a closed oriented surface of genus \(g>1\). A real projective structure on \(\Sigma\) means an \((\mathbb{RP}^2,\mathrm{PGL}(3,\mathbb{R}))\)-structure on \(\Sigma\); its holonomy is called Hitchin if the corresponding representation lies in the Hitchin component. Assume \(\sigma_1\) and \(\sigma_2\) are two real projective structures on \(\Sigma\) with the same Hitchin holonomy, and that their associated grafting data have the same weight type (that is, the same combinatorial weight pattern). A multi-grafting means grafting along a multicurve. Which of the following is the strongest existence statement in this setting?

## Choices
A. If the weight type of \(\sigma_1\) is contained in that of \(\sigma_2\), then \(\sigma_2\) can be obtained from \(\sigma_1\) by a composition of at most \(6g\) multi-graftings.
B. \(\sigma_2\) can be obtained from \(\sigma_1\) by a composition of finitely many multi-graftings.
C. \(\sigma_2\) can be obtained from \(\sigma_1\) by a composition of at most \(6g-6\) multi-graftings.
D. \(\sigma_2\) can be obtained from \(\sigma_1\) by a composition of at most \(6g\) multi-graftings.
E. For any two real projective structures on \(\Sigma\) with the same Hitchin holonomy, even without assuming their grafting data have the same weight type, one can pass from \(\sigma_1\) to \(\sigma_2\) by a composition of at most \(6g\) multi-graftings.
