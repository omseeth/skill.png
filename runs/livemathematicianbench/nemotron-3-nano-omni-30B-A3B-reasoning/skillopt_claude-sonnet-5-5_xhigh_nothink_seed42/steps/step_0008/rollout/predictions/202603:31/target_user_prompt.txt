## Question
Let $(X,\Delta)\to Z\ni o$ be a log Fano fibration germ. Write $\Val_{X,o}^*$ for the nontrivial valuations on $X$ centered over $o$, and for $v\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ write $\mathbf H(v):=\mathbf H(\mathcal F_v)$ for the $\mathbf H$-functional of the filtration induced by $v$. Let $\mathbf H(X,\Delta)$ denote the minimum value of the invariant $\mathbf H(v)$ over $v\in\Val_{X,o}^*$. Which description gives the complete characterization of the minimizers of $\mathbf H$?

## Choices
A. A valuation $v\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ minimizes $\mathbf H$ if and only if $\operatorname{gr}_v R$ is finitely generated and the special degeneration induced by $v$ is K-semistable.
B. Minimizers are unique only up to the degeneration they induce: if $v_1,v_2\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ both satisfy $\mathbf H(v_i)=\mathbf H(X,\Delta)$, then each is quasi-monomial and their associated graded rings define isomorphic K-semistable special degenerations, although $v_1$ and $v_2$ may be distinct valuations.
C. They consist of exactly one valuation: there exists a unique quasi-monomial valuation $v_0\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ such that $\mathbf H(v_0)=\mathbf H(X,\Delta)$.
D. There exists a quasi-monomial valuation $v_0\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ such that $\mathbf H(v_0)=\mathbf H(X,\Delta)$.
E. They form a single quasi-monomial ray: there exists a quasi-monomial valuation $v_0\in\Val_{X,o}^*$ such that $\mathbf H(v)=\mathbf H(X,\Delta)$ if and only if $v=a\,v_0$ for some $a\in\mathbb R_{>0}$; uniqueness holds only after normalizing $v$.
