## Question
Let $K$ be a field, let $a,b,c,d\in K$ satisfy $a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2=-1$, and let $x,y,z$ denote the images of $X,Y,Z$ in the quotient ring
\[
K[x,y,z]:=K[X,Y,Z]/(X^2+Y^2+Z^2-1).
\]
Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
m_1 &= (-2abc+a^2-b^2)y+(a^2c-b^2c+2ab)z,\\
m_2 &= (abc-a^2+c^2+1)x+(bc^2+ac+2b)y+(-ac^2+bc-2a)z-acd,\\
m_3 &= (-a^2c+c^3-ab+c)x+(2ac^2+bc+a)y+(2bc^2-ac+b)z+ad,\\
m_4 &= (-a^2bd-b^3d)z+2a^2bc-a^3+ab^2,\\
m_5 &= (ab^2d+bcd-ad)x+(b^2cd-2abd)y+(-abcd+a^2d+b^2d)z-a^3b+ab^3+abc^2-a^2c,\\
m_6 &= (a^2bd+bc^2d-acd)x-a^2dy+(2b^2cd-abd)z+2a^2b^2-abc+a^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
and set
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
x&y&z\\
m_1&m_2&m_3\\
m_4&m_5&m_6
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Which exact determinant identity holds for $M$ in $K[x,y,z]$?

## Choices
A. \[\det(M)=-2a^2(a^2+d^2)(2abc-a^2+b^2).\]
B. \[\det(M)=2a^2(a^2+c^2)(2abc-a^2+b^2).\]
C. \[\det(M)=2a^2(a^2+d^2)(2abc-a^2+b^2).\]
D. \[\det(M)\in K,\quad\text{indeed }\det(M)=2a^2(a^2+d^2)\,u\text{ for some }u\in K\text{ depending on }a,b,c,d.\]
E. \[\det(M)=2a^2(a^2+d^2)(2abc+a^2-b^2).\]
