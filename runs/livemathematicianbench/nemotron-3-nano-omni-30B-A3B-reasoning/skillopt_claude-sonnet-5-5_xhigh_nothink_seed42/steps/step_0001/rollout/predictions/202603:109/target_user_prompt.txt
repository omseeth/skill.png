## Question
Consider a flat family of hypersurfaces $X_f/\Delta$ over the unit disk $\Delta$, smooth over $\Delta\setminus\{0\}$, with limiting cohomology group $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$. Here the limiting periods mean the entries of the matrix of the comparison isomorphism $\iota_{\infty,K}:\mathbb C\otimes_K H_{\mathrm{dR},\infty,K}\to \mathbb C\otimes_\mathbb Q H_{B,\infty}$ with respect to a $K$-basis of $H_{\mathrm{dR},\infty,K}$ and a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $H_{B,\infty}$. Let $K$ be a subfield of $\mathbb C$ with algebraic closure $\overline K$, let $N_A$ be an integer, let $\operatorname{Fan}(A)$ be a fan with skeleton $\operatorname{Sk}(\operatorname{Fan}(A))$, and let $\mathbf w_f$ be the associated weight vector. Assume that $\mathbf w_f\notin \operatorname{Sk}(\operatorname{Fan}(A))$. Which of the following is the strongest statement that holds for every such object about its limiting periods?

## Choices
A. All limiting periods of $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$ belong to the $\overline K$-subalgebra of $\mathbb C$ generated by $(2\pi i)^{\pm1}$, by logarithms $\log \alpha$ with $\alpha\in \overline K^\times$, by $\Gamma(j/N_A)^{\pm1}$ for integers $j$ with $0<j<N_A$, and by Dirichlet $L$-values $L(k,\chi)$ for integers $k\ge 2$ and Dirichlet characters $\chi$ whose conductors divide $N_A$, even without the hypothesis $\mathbf w_f\notin \operatorname{Sk}(\operatorname{Fan}(A))$.
B. All limiting periods of $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$ belong to the $\overline K$-subalgebra of $\mathbb C$ generated by $(2\pi i)^{\pm1}$, by logarithms $\log \alpha$ with $\alpha\in \overline K^\times$, by $\Gamma(j/N_A)^{\pm1}$ for integers $j$ with $0<j<N_A$, and by Dirichlet $L$-values $L(k,\chi)$ for integers $k\ge 2$ and Dirichlet characters $\chi$ whose conductors divide $N_A$.
C. All limiting periods of $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$ belong to the $\overline K$-subalgebra of $\mathbb C$ generated by $(2\pi i)^{\pm1}$, by logarithms $\log \alpha$ with $\alpha\in \overline K^\times$, by $\Gamma(j/N_A)^{\pm1}$ for integers $j$ with $0<j<N_A$, and by the Riemann zeta values $\zeta(k)$ for integers $k\ge 2$.
D. There exists a finitely generated $\overline K$-subalgebra $R\subset \mathbb C$, contained in the $\overline K$-subalgebra of $\mathbb C$ generated by $(2\pi i)^{\pm1}$, by logarithms $\log \alpha$ with $\alpha\in \overline K^\times$, by $\Gamma(j/N_A)^{\pm1}$ for integers $j$ with $0<j<N_A$, and by Dirichlet $L$-values $L(k,\chi)$ for integers $k\ge 2$ and Dirichlet characters $\chi$ whose conductors divide $N_A$, such that all limiting periods of $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$ belong to $R$.
E. All limiting periods of $H^{n-2}_\infty(X_f/\Delta)_K$ belong to the $\overline K$-subalgebra of $\mathbb C$ generated by $(2\pi i)^{\pm1}$, by logarithms $\log \alpha$ with $\alpha\in \overline K^\times$, by $\Gamma(j/N_A)^{\pm1}$ for integers $j$ with $0<j<N_A$, and by Dirichlet $L$-values $L(k,\chi)$ only for even integers $k\ge 2$ and Dirichlet characters $\chi$ whose conductors divide $N_A$.
