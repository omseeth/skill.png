## Question
Let \(\mathbf V\) be a pseudovariety of semigroups (that is, a class of finite semigroups closed under finite direct products, subsemigroups, and homomorphic images), and assume that every group in \(\mathbf V\) is solvable. Here \(N\) denotes the size of the ambient finite semigroup, and a straight-line program is a sequence that builds elements from the given generators using semigroup multiplication, with length equal to the number of multiplication steps. Which statement is equivalent to the assertion that \(\mathbf V\) admits straight-line programs of length \(\mathcal O((\log N)^2)\)?

## Choices
A. \(\mathbf V\) affords efficient compression, i.e. admits straight-line programs of length \(\operatorname{polylog} N\).
B. \(\mathbf V\) contains neither \(\mathbf{LRB}\) nor \(\mathbf{RRB}\), and contains no nonsolvable group.
C. \(\mathbf V\) admits straight-line programs of length \(\mathcal O(\log N)\).
D. \(\mathbf V\) admits straight-line programs of width \(\mathcal O(1)\) and length \(\mathcal O(\log N)\).
E. \(\mathbf V\) admits straight-line programs of width \(2\) and length \(\mathcal O(\operatorname{polylog} N)\).
