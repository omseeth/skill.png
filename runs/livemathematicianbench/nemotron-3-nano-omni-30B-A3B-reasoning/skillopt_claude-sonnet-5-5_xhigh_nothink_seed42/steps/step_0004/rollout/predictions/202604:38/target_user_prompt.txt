## Question
Let \(\pi:X\to \mathbb D^*\) be the Fermat family over the punctured unit disc \(\mathbb D^*=\{t\in\mathbb C:0<|t|<1\}\), whose fibre in \(\mathbb P^2\) is defined by
\[
xyz-t(x^3+y^3+z^3)=0.
\]
Let \(L\) be the restriction to \(X\) of the anticanonical line bundle \(-K_{\mathbb P^2}\). Fix an integer \(k\ge 1\), and let \(H^0(X,L^k)\) denote the vector space of global sections of \(L^k\) on the total space \(X\). Which of the following is the strongest existence statement that holds in this setting?

## Choices
A. For each integer \(k\ge 1\), the monomial sections of \(H^0(X,L^k)\) inherited from \(\mathbb P^2\) already form a valuatively independent basis.
B. The vector space \(H^0(X,L^k)\) admits a basis that is valuatively independent.
C. For each integer \(k\ge 1\), the vector space \(H^0(X,L^k)\) admits a basis that is locally valuatively independent on an open cover of \(\operatorname{Sk}(X,\pi)\), and this local independence automatically implies valuative independence on all of \(\operatorname{Sk}(X,\pi)\).
D. For each integer \(k\ge 1\), the vector space \(H^0(X,L^k)\) admits a basis that is locally valuatively independent on sufficiently small open subsets of \(\operatorname{Sk}(X,\pi)\).
E. One can choose valuatively independent bases \(B_m\subset H^0(X,L^m)\) for every \(m\ge 1\) such that \(B_k\cdot B_\ell\subseteq B_{k+\ell}\) for all \(k,\ell\ge 1\).
