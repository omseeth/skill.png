## Question
Let \(\Omega\subset \mathbb{R}^n\) be open, and let \(f:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}\) be specularly Fr\'echet differentiable. Write \(\widehat{D}f(\mathbf{x})\) for its specular Fr\'echet derivative at \(\mathbf{x}\), \(\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)\) for the specular G\^ateaux derivative at \(\mathbf x\) in direction \(\mathbf v\), \(\partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x)\) for the derivative in the \(i\)-th coordinate direction, and \(\sg f(\mathbf x)\) for the specular gradient. Here \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle\) (equivalently \(\cdot\)) denotes the standard dot product on \(\mathbb R^n\), and \(\|\cdot\|_{\mathbb R^n}\) denotes the Euclidean norm. Which of the following is the strongest valid conclusion about these objects and about local extrema of \(f\)?

## Choices
A. For every \(\mathbf x\in\Omega\), \(f\) is specularly G\^ateaux differentiable at \(\mathbf x\) in every nonzero direction \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\), and
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)=\langle \widehat{D}f(\mathbf x),\mathbf v\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x)=\bigl(\partial^{\sd}_{x_1}f(\mathbf x),\partial^{\sd}_{x_2}f(\mathbf x),\ldots,\partial^{\sd}_{x_n}f(\mathbf x)\bigr).
\]
If \(\mathbf x^*\in\Omega\) is a local minimizer or a local maximizer of \(f\), then
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x^*)=\mathbf 0,
\]
equivalently \(\sg f(\mathbf x^*)\cdot \mathbf v=0\) for every \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\), and in particular
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x^*)=0\quad(1\le i\le n).
\]
B. For every \(\mathbf x\in\Omega\), \(f\) is specularly G\^ateaux differentiable at \(\mathbf x\) in every nonzero direction \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\), and
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)=\left\langle \widehat{D}f(\mathbf x),\frac{\mathbf v}{\|\mathbf v\|_{\mathbb R^n}}\right\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x)=\bigl(\partial^{\sd}_{x_1}f(\mathbf x),\partial^{\sd}_{x_2}f(\mathbf x),\ldots,\partial^{\sd}_{x_n}f(\mathbf x)\bigr).
\]
If \(\mathbf x^*\in\Omega\) is a local minimizer or a local maximizer of \(f\), then for every \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\),
\[
\bigl|\sg f(\mathbf x^*)\cdot \mathbf v\bigr|\le \|\mathbf v\|_{\mathbb R^n},
\]
and in particular,
\[
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n \partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x^*)\right|\le \sqrt n.
\]
C. For every \(\mathbf x\in\Omega\), \(f\) is specularly G\^ateaux differentiable at \(\mathbf x\) in every nonzero direction \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\), and
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)=\langle \widehat{D}f(\mathbf x),\mathbf v\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x)=\bigl(\partial^{\sd}_{x_1}f(\mathbf x),\partial^{\sd}_{x_2}f(\mathbf x),\ldots,\partial^{\sd}_{x_n}f(\mathbf x)\bigr).
\]
If \(\mathbf x^*\in\Omega\) is a local minimizer or a local maximizer of \(f\), then for every \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\),
\[
\bigl|\sg f(\mathbf x^*)\cdot \mathbf v\bigr|\le \|\mathbf v\|_{\mathbb R^n},
\]
and in particular,
\[
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n \partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x^*)\right|\le 1.
\]
D. For every \(\mathbf x\in\Omega\), \(f\) is specularly G\^ateaux differentiable at \(\mathbf x\) in every nonzero direction \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\), and
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)=\langle \widehat{D}f(\mathbf x),\mathbf v\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x)=\bigl(\partial^{\sd}_{x_1}f(\mathbf x),\partial^{\sd}_{x_2}f(\mathbf x),\ldots,\partial^{\sd}_{x_n}f(\mathbf x)\bigr).
\]
If \(\mathbf x^*\in\Omega\) is a local minimizer or a local maximizer of \(f\), then for every \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\),
\[
\bigl|\sg f(\mathbf x^*)\cdot \mathbf v\bigr|\le \|\mathbf v\|_{\mathbb R^n},
\]
and in particular,
\[
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n \partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x^*)\right|\le \sqrt n.
\]
E. For every \(\mathbf x\in\Omega\), \(f\) is specularly G\^ateaux differentiable at \(\mathbf x\) in every nonzero direction \(\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{\mathbf 0\}\), and
\[
\partial^{\sd}_{\mathbf v}f(\mathbf x)=\langle \widehat{D}f(\mathbf x),\mathbf v\rangle.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sg f(\mathbf x)=\bigl(\partial^{\sd}_{x_1}f(\mathbf x),\partial^{\sd}_{x_2}f(\mathbf x),\ldots,\partial^{\sd}_{x_n}f(\mathbf x)\bigr).
\]
If \(\mathbf x^*\in\Omega\) is a local minimizer or a local maximizer of \(f\), then for the specific vector \(\mathbf 1=(1,1,\ldots,1)\in\mathbb R^n\),
\[
\bigl|\sg f(\mathbf x^*)\cdot \mathbf 1\bigr|\le \sqrt n,
\]
and hence
\[
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n \partial^{\sd}_{x_i}f(\mathbf x^*)\right|\le \sqrt n.
\]
