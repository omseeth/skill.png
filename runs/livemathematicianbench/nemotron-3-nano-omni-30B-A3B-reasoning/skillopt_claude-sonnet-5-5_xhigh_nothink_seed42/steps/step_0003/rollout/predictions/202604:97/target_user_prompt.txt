## Question
Let \((NS_\kappa)\) denote the one-dimensional periodic non-isentropic Navier--Stokes system for a perfect gas on \(\mathbb R_+\times \mathbb T\), with unknowns density \(\rho\), velocity \(u\), and temperature \(\theta\):
\[
\partial_t \rho+\partial_x(\rho u)=0,
\]
\[
\partial_t(\rho u)+\partial_x(\rho u^2)=\partial_x(\mu\partial_x u-R\rho\theta),
\]
\[
\partial_t\!\left(\frac{\rho u^2}{2}+c_v\rho\theta\right)+\partial_x\!\left(\frac{\rho u^3}{2}+c_v\rho\theta u\right)
=\partial_x(\mu(\partial_x u)u-R\rho\theta u)+\partial_x(\kappa\partial_x\theta),
\]
where \(\mathbb R_+=[0,\infty)\) and \(\mathbb T\) is the one-dimensional torus. Let \(\overline\kappa\ge \underline\kappa>0\). Suppose initial data \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) on \(\mathbb T\) satisfy, for almost every \(x\in\mathbb T\),
\[
\underline{\rho_0}\le \rho_0(x)\le \overline{\rho_0},
\]
and
\[
\int_{\mathbb T} \rho_0 u_0^2+\int_{\mathbb T} (\partial_x u_0)^2\le C_0.
\]
Here, a global "a la Hoff" solution of \((NS_\kappa)\) means a weak solution \((\rho,u,\theta)\in L^2_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R_+\times\mathbb T)^3\) such that on every time interval \([0,T]\) it satisfies positive lower and upper bounds for \(\rho\) and \(\theta\), together with the additional Hoff-type a priori estimates required in the definition. What is the strongest existence-and-uniqueness statement that follows for \((NS_\kappa)\)?

## Choices
A. For every \(\kappa\) with \(\underline\kappa\le \kappa\le \overline\kappa\), the system \((NS_\kappa)\) with initial condition \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) admits a unique global "a la Hoff" solution, and the corresponding lower and upper bounds for \(\rho\) and \(\theta\), as well as the Hoff-type control constants, may be chosen uniformly for all \(T>0\).
B. For every \(\kappa\) with \(\underline\kappa\le \kappa\le \overline\kappa\), the system \((NS_\kappa)\) with initial condition \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) admits a unique global weak solution in \(L^2_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R_+\times\mathbb T)^3\), and every such weak solution automatically satisfies the "a la Hoff" bounds.
C. For every \(\kappa\) with \(\underline\kappa\le \kappa\le \overline\kappa\), the system \((NS_\kappa)\) with initial condition \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) admits a unique global "a la Hoff" solution; moreover, for each \(T>0\), the lower and upper bounds for \(\rho\) and \(\theta\), together with the Hoff-type control constants, can be chosen to depend only on \(C_0,c_v,\gamma,\overline\kappa,\mu\), and \(T\).
D. For every \(\kappa\) with \(\underline\kappa\le \kappa\le \overline\kappa\), the system \((NS_\kappa)\) with initial condition \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) admits a unique global "a la Hoff" solution.
E. For every \(\kappa\) with \(\underline\kappa\le \kappa\le \overline\kappa\), the system \((NS_\kappa)\) with initial condition \((\rho_0,u_0,\theta_0)\) admits a global "a la Hoff" solution.
