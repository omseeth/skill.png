## Question
Let \(\|x\|\) denote the distance from a real number \(x\) to the nearest integer. Fix integers \(d\ge 2\) and \(k\ge 1\). Which of the following is the strongest statement asserting the existence of a constant, depending only on \(d\), \(k\), and any indicated \(\varepsilon>0\), that works uniformly for all \(N\ge 2\) and all \(\beta\in\mathbb R\)?

## Choices
A. For every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists a constant \(C_{d,k,\varepsilon}>0\), depending only on \(d\), \(k\), and \(\varepsilon\), such that for every \(N\ge 2\), there exist integers \(b_1,\dots,b_k\) with \(1\le b_1,\dots,b_k\le N\) for which
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k b_j^{1/d}-\beta\right\|\le C_{d,k,\varepsilon}N^{-k/d+\varepsilon}
\quad\text{for every }\beta\in\mathbb R.
\]
B. There exists a constant \(C_{d,k}>0\), depending only on \(d\) and \(k\), such that for every \(\varepsilon>0\), every \(N\ge 2\), and every \(\beta\in\mathbb R\), there exist integers \(b_1,\dots,b_k\) with \(1\le b_1,\dots,b_k\le N\) and
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k b_j^{1/d}-\beta\right\|\le C_{d,k}N^{-k/d+\varepsilon}.
\]
C. For every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists a constant \(C_{d,k,\varepsilon}>0\), depending only on \(d\), \(k\), and \(\varepsilon\), such that for every sufficiently large \(N\) and every \(\beta\in\mathbb R\), there exist integers \(b_1,\dots,b_k\) with \(1\le b_1,\dots,b_k\le N\) and
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k b_j^{1/d}-\beta\right\|\le C_{d,k,\varepsilon}N^{-k/d+\varepsilon}.
\]
D. For every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists a constant \(C_{d,k,\varepsilon}>0\), depending only on \(d\), \(k\), and \(\varepsilon\), such that for every \(N\ge 2\) and every \(\beta\in\mathbb R\), there exist integers \(b_1,\dots,b_k\) with \(1\le b_1,\dots,b_k\le N\) and
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k b_j^{1/d}-\beta\right\|\le C_{d,k,\varepsilon}N^{-k/d+\varepsilon}.
\]
E. There exists a constant \(C_{d,k}>0\), depending only on \(d\) and \(k\), such that for every \(N\ge 2\) and every \(\beta\in\mathbb R\), there exist integers \(b_1,\dots,b_k\) with \(1\le b_1,\dots,b_k\le N\) and
\[
\left\|\sum_{j=1}^k b_j^{1/d}-\beta\right\|\le C_{d,k}N^{-k/d}.
\]
