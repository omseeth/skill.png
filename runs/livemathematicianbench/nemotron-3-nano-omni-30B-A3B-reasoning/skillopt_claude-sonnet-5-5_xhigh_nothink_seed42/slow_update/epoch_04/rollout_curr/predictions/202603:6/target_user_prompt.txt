## Question
For a finite group $G$, let $\mathbb{P}_{ch}(G)$ denote the proportion (up to isomorphism) of chiral orientably-regular maps among all orientably-regular maps with automorphism group $G$. Let $\mathcal{S}_n$ and $\mathcal{A}_n$ be the symmetric and alternating groups of degree $n$, respectively. As $n\to\infty$, which of the following is the strongest quantitative estimate for the deficits of these proportions from $1$?

## Choices
A. Both deficits satisfy
\[
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n)\sim \tfrac{3}{4}\,4^n n^{-0.25n},
\qquad
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)\sim \tfrac{1}{4}\,4^n n^{-0.25n}.
\]
B. There exists an absolute constant \(C>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n)\le C\,3^n n^{-0.25n},
\qquad
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)\le C\,3^n n^{-0.25n}.
\]
C. Both deficits satisfy
\[
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n),\; 1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)\in O\!\left(4^n\,n^{-0.5n}\right).
\]
D. Both deficits satisfy
\[
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n),\; 1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)\in O\!\left(4^n\,n^{-0.25n}\right).
\]
E. Both deficits satisfy
\[
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n)\to 0,
\qquad
1-\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)\to 0,
\]
so in particular
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{S}_n)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb{P}_{ch}(\mathcal{A}_n)=1.
\]
