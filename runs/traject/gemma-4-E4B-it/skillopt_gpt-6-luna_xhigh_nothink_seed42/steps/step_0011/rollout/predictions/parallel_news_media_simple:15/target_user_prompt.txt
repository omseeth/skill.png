## Query
The search began with a News API query for recent articles about 'tech industry AI regulation' published since October 2023, limiting results to English-language content from major publishers (CNN, BBC, The Verge, Wired, and TechCrunch) with a request for 15 articles. The search was then expanded to developer-focused content by accessing top-rated articles of all time from dev.to, followed by retrieving the latest articles from the same platform to get more current perspectives. The search scope was further broadened to include international news by retrieving Arabic-language content from France24 Arabic. A specific politician with ID '437c7e15-c66a-4b46-af9b-07e9b1317190' was looked up through the Goverlytics service, possibly to examine their stance on AI regulation. Finally, the BizToc Pages endpoint was accessed to identify available business news categories, potentially to explore additional AI regulation coverage in business contexts.
