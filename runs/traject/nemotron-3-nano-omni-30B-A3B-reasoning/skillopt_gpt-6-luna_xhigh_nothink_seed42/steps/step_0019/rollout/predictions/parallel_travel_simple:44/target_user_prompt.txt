### Flightera Flight Data: airlineFlights
Provider: Flightera Flight Data — Flight status, on-time performance and statistics by Flightera

Description: Retrieves a list of airline flights sorted by scheduled departure time, using a start time for pagination and returning local departure dates for subsequent queries.
