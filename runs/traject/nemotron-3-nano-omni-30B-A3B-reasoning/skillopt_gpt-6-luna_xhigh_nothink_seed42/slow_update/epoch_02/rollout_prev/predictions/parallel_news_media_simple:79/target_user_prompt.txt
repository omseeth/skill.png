## Query
The search began by querying the News API for articles about the 'UK general election', specifically targeting sources from Great Britain, sorting results by relevancy, and requesting up to 20 articles. To gather additional financial perspective, the search continued with a query to BizToc's business and finance news index for articles about 'UK election economic policy debate' from the past 14 days. Finally, the search checked which geographic regions were available for news coverage by accessing the News Network's region list feature, providing a complete picture of where news content could be sourced globally.
