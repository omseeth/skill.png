## Query
Analyze multiple temporary email addresses (tempuser@mailinator.com, quickaccount@10minutemail.com, and signup2024@guerrillamail.com) to determine if they are disposable emails and verify their validity. First, check each email using the Alpha Email Verification tool to identify if they are disposable emails from known temporary email domains. Then, retrieve and examine the MX (Mail Exchange) records for each email domain using the Email Existence Validator tool to verify their mail server configurations and determine if they are legitimate functioning email addresses.
