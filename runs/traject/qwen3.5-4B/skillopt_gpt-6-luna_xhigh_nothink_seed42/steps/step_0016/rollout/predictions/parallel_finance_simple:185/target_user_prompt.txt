## Query
I analyzed Ethereum (ETH) price data using multiple cryptocurrency tools. First, I retrieved daily historical data for ETH in USD using Alpha Vantage's DIGITAL_CURRENCY_DAILY endpoint, which provides comprehensive price information refreshed daily at midnight UTC. Then, I examined recent trading activity for the ETHUSDT pair by pulling 500 recent trades from Bybit's Public Trading Records. Finally, I analyzed mark price movements for ETHUSDT futures on Bybit's linear perpetual market, looking at hourly (60-minute) klines between March 13, 2023 (timestamp 1678665600000) and March 15, 2023 (timestamp 1678838400000). This combination of tools allowed me to examine Ethereum's price history, recent trading patterns, and specific price movements during a targeted timeframe.
