## Check Candidates Against the Whole Clue
Before answering, test a candidate against every defining descriptor and relationship in the clue; matching one salient word or a nearby fact is not enough. When evidence snippets compete, prefer the one that matches the clue's full category and qualifiers, including its timeframe when relevant. Treat page, file, and source headings as metadata rather than answers; if a passage explicitly marks a clue's answer, use that keyed answer.

## Use Evidence Context Carefully
A snippet that repeats the clue can be a useful anchor, but check its nearby context to find the answer rather than treating the repeated clue text as the answer. Corroborate the identified entity with factual evidence when available, and do not rely on incidental options or unrelated nearby material.

## Follow the Output Format
Honor the requested final-answer format exactly, including required tags, structure, or labels. Keep analysis and unrequested explanation out of the final answer.
